Club Guidelines

How our little club runs — agreed on by all of us.

  1. Third Wednesday of the month

    Meetings will be held the third Wednesday of every month.

  2. 7–9 pm

    We meet from 7 to 9 pm. Book discussion runs 7:30–8:30, with the time before and after for socializing.

  3. No meetings in December

    Enjoy the holidays with a long cozy book!

  4. Any genre is welcome

    The type of books to be read is left open. No genre is excluded from being brought to the group for a vote.

  5. We rotate

    Meetings rotate among members’. A local restaurant or other public setting is an option if weather or other circumstances prevent meeting at a home.

  6. Everyone hosts

    Each person signs up to host a meeting (see the 2027 Sign-Up page). The host selects 3–5 books to present, and the group votes on them the month before that host’s meeting.

  7. Majority rules

    Majority vote prevails for all group decisions — for example, changes to the group format, membership issues and book selection.

  8. The host moderates

    The monthly host moderates the book discussion. Book guides are fine to use and are often available online.

  9. Drinks and snacks

    Alcohol and snacks are desired at each meeting.

  10. 15 members max

    The group is limited to 15 members.

  11. Checking in

    If a member misses 3 meetings in a row, someone will reach out to see whether she’d like to continue.

  12. New members

    New members can join when an opening exists. This will be discussed at meetings or by email.
